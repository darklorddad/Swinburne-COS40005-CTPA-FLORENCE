BioTective: AI-Powered Chronic Disease Monitoring

A predictive platform for proactive digital healthcare.

Introduction

A brief overview of the BioTective project's purpose and its significance in the evolving landscape of digital health, focusing on leveraging artificial intelligence for proactive chronic disease management. Our platform aims to shift the paradigm from reactive to predictive healthcare, providing clinicians and patients with actionable insights to prevent adverse health events.

The Problem

Chronic diseases are a leading cause of death and disability worldwide, yet current monitoring systems are often reactive and fragmented. This section highlights the challenges in traditional healthcare and the urgent need for innovative, data-driven solutions.

monitoring

Reactive Monitoring

Traditional methods often rely on periodic check-ups, missing critical data points between visits and delaying intervention.

data_thresholding

Data Overload

Clinicians are overwhelmed with raw data from wearables and sensors, lacking the tools to extract meaningful, actionable insights efficiently.

Conclusion

Future Works

Our roadmap includes expanding the platform to cover a broader range of chronic conditions, enhancing model personalization using federated learning, and conducting large-scale clinical trials to validate long-term patient outcomes and cost-effectiveness. We also aim to develop a patient-facing application to empower individuals in their own health management journey.
