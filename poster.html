BioTective

AI-Powered Chronic Disease Monitoring

A predictive platform for proactive digital healthcare.

Introduction

This project, for BioTective Sdn Bhd, is a prototype AI-enabled digital health platform for chronic disease monitoring. In Malaysia, NCDs are the leading cause of death, straining a healthcare system designed for acute care [1]. The platform integrates multi-source health data (glucose, diet, activity) and uses AI to provide actionable insights, transforming chronic disease management into a proactive, data-driven partnership.

The Problem

Patients lack a unified platform for fragmented health data that provides real-time, personalised guidance. This leads to:

  • personal_injury Patient Pain: Fragmented records, difficulty with self-management, and the burden of long-term illness [3].
  • medical_services Clinician Pain: High patient ratios, lack of integrated data, and inefficient management in an acute care model [1].

Goal: A high-fidelity prototype of an AI-Enabled Digital Health Platform.

Literature Review

A competitive analysis of the Malaysian digital health market reveals a gap [2, 4]. Existing platforms are reactive (DoctorOnCall), focus on wellness (BookDoc), or use human coaching (Naluri Health). There is an unmet need for a scalable, automated platform for proactive, continuous chronic disease management [4].

Conclusion

This project addresses a critical gap by prototyping an AI-powered platform for proactive chronic disease management. It empowers patients and provides clinicians with an "attention funnel" to prioritise high-risk individuals. The prototype validates the technical feasibility and strategic value, laying the groundwork for a commercially viable product to improve health outcomes.
