BioTective: AI-Powered Chronic Disease Monitoring

A predictive platform for proactive digital healthcare.

Introduction

This project, undertaken for BioTective Sdn Bhd, involves the prototype development of an AI-enabled digital health platform for chronic disease monitoring. In Malaysia, chronic, non-communicable diseases (NCDs) are the leading cause of death and disability, placing an immense strain on a healthcare system primarily designed for acute, episodic care [1]. Patients often struggle with fragmented health information and a lack of real-time monitoring. This project aims to create a prototype that integrates multi-source health data (e.g., glucose levels, diet, activity) and uses Artificial Intelligence to provide actionable insights. The platform is designed to facilitate timely interventions by both patients and clinicians, transforming chronic disease management from a reactive process into a proactive, data-driven partnership.

The Problem

The core problem is that chronic disease patients lack a unified platform that integrates their fragmented health data and provides real-time, actionable, and personalised guidance. Existing digital health solutions often act as simple data repositories and do not fully leverage AI to detect early warning signs or provide context-aware recommendations.

This leads to significant pain points for both patients and clinicians:

  • arrow_right Patient Pain Points: Fragmented care records, difficulty with self-management, low health literacy, and the financial and psychological burden of long-term illness [3].
  • arrow_right Clinician Pain Points: Systemic overload due to high patient-to-doctor ratios, lack of integrated data for decision-making, and the inefficiency of managing chronic conditions within an acute care model [1].

The project's primary goal is to design, develop, and deliver a high-fidelity prototype of an AI-Enabled Digital Health Platform that addresses these issues by the end of the semester.

Literature Review

The Malaysian digital health market is expanding rapidly, yet a competitive analysis reveals a significant market gap [2, 4]. Existing platforms like DoctorOnCall and Doc2Us excel at transactional, on-demand services such as teleconsultations and e-prescriptions but are fundamentally reactive. They serve patients who actively seek care but do not offer continuous, proactive monitoring. Other platforms like BookDoc focus on general wellness and gamified activity tracking rather than clinical disease management. The closest competitor, Naluri Health, offers structured programmes for chronic disease management but relies on a human-coaching model. While effective, this approach is resource-intensive and faces challenges in scalability. The analysis concludes that there is an unmet need for a scalable, automated platform that fosters a continuous, proactive care relationship with the patient, moving beyond the episodic interactions that currently dominate the market [4].

Methodology

The solution is a high-fidelity prototype built on a modern, decoupled system architecture designed for scalability, security, and rapid development.

  • architecture System Architecture:

    The solution is built on a microservices-style architecture to separate concerns. This includes a Platform Service which comprises the frontend application. A dedicated Data Service acts as the sole gateway to the Supabase backend, managing all authentication and secure data storage operations. The LLM Chatbot Service handles conversational logic, communicating with the frontend to manage user interaction and with the Data Service to retrieve patient-specific information for context.

  • layers Technology Stack:
    • Frontend: Flutter is used for its ability to create a high-performance, consistent user interface across mobile and web from a single codebase.
    • Backend: Python with the FastAPI framework was chosen for its high performance and unrivalled ecosystem of AI/ML libraries.
    • Database: Supabase is used as the Backend-as-a-Service (BaaS) platform, leveraging its underlying PostgreSQL database with Row-Level Security (RLS) to ensure strict data segregation.
  • psychology AI Core:

    The AI core is implemented as an autonomous agent using the LangChain framework. The system leverages the LLM's tool-calling capabilities. The agent is provided with a set of 'tools'—custom Python functions—that allow it to interact with the Data Service to fetch live patient data, such as recent glucose readings or diet history. While the initial prompt is constructed with relevant context to streamline the process, the agent retains the ability to autonomously use these tools to gather more information as needed to form its reasoning and generate a personalised response.

Conclusion

This project addresses a critical gap in the Malaysian digital health ecosystem by developing a prototype for an AI-powered platform focused on proactive and continuous chronic disease management. By integrating patient data, providing personalised AI-driven insights, and automating engagement, the platform aims to empower patients in their self-management journey. Simultaneously, it provides clinicians with an intelligent "attention funnel" that prioritises high-risk individuals, enhancing efficiency and enabling timely interventions. The successful delivery of this prototype will validate the technical feasibility and strategic value of this approach, laying the groundwork for a commercially viable product that can significantly improve health outcomes for millions living with chronic disease.

Future Works

Upon successful completion of the prototype, future development could focus on:

  • integration_instructions Clinical Integration: Pursue integration with existing Hospital Information Systems (HIS) and Electronic Health Records (EHRs) using interoperability standards like HL7 FHIR.
  • expand_content Expansion to Other Chronic Diseases: Adapt the modular architecture to support other conditions such as hypertension, COPD, and chronic kidney disease.
  • analytics Advanced Predictive Analytics: Incorporate predictive machine learning models to forecast future health risks, such as hypoglycaemic events or hospital readmissions, enabling preventative interventions.
  • video_chat Telemedicine Integration: Integrate secure video consultation capabilities to allow clinicians to initiate a virtual visit directly from the dashboard upon receiving a high-risk alert.
