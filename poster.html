AI-ENABLED DIGITAL HEALTH PLATFORM FOR CHRONIC DISEASE MONITORING

BioTective: A Proactive Approach to Chronic Disease

Daniel Tiong, Alif Harriz, Ashley Jong, Basil Agas, Edison Ho

Introduction

This project, for BioTective Sdn Bhd, involves prototyping an AI-enabled digital health platform for chronic disease monitoring. In Malaysia, non-communicable diseases (NCDs) are the leading cause of death, straining a healthcare system designed for acute care [1]. Patients struggle with fragmented health data and lack real-time monitoring. This project aims to build a prototype that integrates health data (glucose, diet, activity) and uses AI to provide actionable insights, facilitating timely interventions by patients and clinicians and shifting care from reactive to proactive.

Problem

Chronic disease patients lack a unified platform that integrates their fragmented health data to provide real-time, personalised guidance. Existing digital solutions often act as simple data logs and fail to leverage AI for early warnings or context-aware recommendations.

Conclusion

This project addresses a critical gap in Malaysian digital health by prototyping an AI-powered platform for proactive chronic disease management. By integrating data, providing personalised AI insights, and automating engagement, the platform empowers patients in self-management. It also provides clinicians an "attention funnel" to prioritise high-risk individuals, enhancing efficiency. This prototype validates the approach, laying the groundwork for a product to improve health outcomes.

Future Works

  • Integration with EHR/HIS systems via HL7 FHIR.
  • Expansion to other chronic conditions (e.g., Hypertension, COPD).
  • Incorporate predictive analytics for events like hypoglycemia.
  • Integrate telemedicine for real-time clinician intervention.
