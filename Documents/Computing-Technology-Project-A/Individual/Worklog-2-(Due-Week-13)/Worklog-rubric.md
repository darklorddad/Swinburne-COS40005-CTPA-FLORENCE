# Worklog Rubric

| Criteria | Ratings | Ratings | Ratings | Pts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Task completion** | **4 to >3.0 pts**<br>**Good**<br>Tasks completed demonstrate sufficient contribution to the project, all related descriptions are provided about the tasks | **3 to >1.0 pts**<br>**Satisfactory**<br>Tasks completed demonstrate some contribution to the project, tasks are described with details | **1 to >0 pts**<br>**Unsatisfactory**<br>Either tasks completed are not sufficient or tasks are not described in detail | **4 pts** |
| **Tasks planned** | **2 to >1.0 pts**<br>**Satisfactory**<br>Tasks planned for the following week are sufficient and consistent with team plan | **1 to >0 pts**<br>**Unsatisfactory**<br>Tasks planned are not consistent with team plan or are not sufficient | | **2 pts** |
| **Reflection** | **2 to >1.0 pts**<br>**Satisfactory**<br>Key learning, resources and issues (if any) are described in detail. | **1 to >0 pts**<br>**Unsatisfactory**<br>Incomplete/insufficient summary of reflection | | **2 pts** |
| **Worklog summary** | **2 to >1.0 pts**<br>**Satisfactory**<br>The high-level summary reflects that the student's contribution is acceptable in regards to this week and is in line with the plan this semester | **1 to >0 pts**<br>**Unsatisfactory**<br>The high-level summary does not reflect sufficient contribution in regards to this week and is not in line with the plan this semester | | **2 pts** |

**Total Points: 10**
