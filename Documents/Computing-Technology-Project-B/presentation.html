The Clinician Experience

The "Attention Funnel"

Doctors don't need more data points; they need filtered insights.

  • Risk-Based Prioritization: Automatically scores and pushes high-risk patients to the top of the queue.
  • Actionable Alerts: Instantly surfaces anomalies like missed medications or sustained glucose spikes.
  • Population Health: Empowers clinicians to focus limited resources on patients requiring immediate intervention.

Technical Architecture

Frontend

Flutter (Dart)

Cross-platform framework delivering a native, fluid experience on Web and Mobile from a single codebase.

Backend

FastAPI & Supabase

Python-based microservices for high performance, backed by a robust PostgreSQL BaaS infrastructure.

AI Engine

LangChain (RAG)

Large Action Models powered by Retrieval-Augmented Generation to ensure clinical accuracy and prevent hallucinations.

Do No Harm: Security & Ethics

Trust is the foundational currency of Digital Health.

  • PDPA 2010 Compliant: Built from day one with strict data governance, privacy policies, and encryption.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Absolute database segregation. Patients only see their data; Clinicians only see assigned patients.
  • AI Explainability: The "Glass Box" approach. Recommendations transparently cite the specific data points that triggered them.
  • Human Oversight: AI acts strictly as a decision-support tool. The ultimate medical authority remains with the Clinician.
