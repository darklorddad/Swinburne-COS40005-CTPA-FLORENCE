our solution

Meet FLORENCE.

An AI-powered health companion that sits with patients between doctor visits — monitoring, guiding, and alerting. Every single day.

Monitor

Patients log vitals, meals, and medications daily. FLORENCE watches for patterns others would miss.

Guide

AI generates a personalized daily insight and 2–3 priority actions — every morning, based on real data.

Alert

When something is off, FLORENCE notifies the patient and their clinician immediately — before it escalates.

2026
clinician role · technical

Built for speed.
Secured for healthcare.

Secure Architecture (Zero Direct DB)
The clinician frontend never touches the database directly. All traffic routes through a secure middleware REST API, ensuring enterprise-grade data protection.
Standardized, Dynamic Analytics
From BMI to Glucose trends, charts dynamically scale to prevent data overlap. Color-coded status pills flag "Above Target" readings instantly.
Unified Ecosystem UX
Clinician interfaces share the identical visual language, color-coding, and hierarchy as the patient app, creating one cohesive, branded platform.
Dynamic Risk Engine
The patient list isn't alphabetical—it's priority-based. High-risk patients automatically float to the top based on real-time continuous data flow.
